How It Works
Built on probability. Not hype.

Every fixture is scored using historical goal data. No narratives, no gut feelings just clean algorithmic output.

01

Data Input

Historical goal scoring data is fed into the GSP engine for each team in every fixture.

02

Algorithm Runs

The goal market algorithm calculates per-team and combined scoring probabilities.

03

Risk Tagged

Each fixture is tagged Low, Medium, or High risk for Over 0.5 and Over 1.5 markets.

04

You Decide

You get clear signals to engage or skip. No pressure, no fake sure-wins.
